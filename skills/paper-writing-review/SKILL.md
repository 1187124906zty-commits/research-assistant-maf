# Writing Review

Read the assigned manuscript, question and available original evidence. Identify article type, audience and scope before judging structure. Disclose missing evidence and prior exposure to the producer's interpretation. Review actual prose and displays, not the worker's summary.

Reconstruct the argument in plain language: what is studied, why this question follows, how the design or reasoning answers it, what the evidence establishes, and what remains uncertain. Locate where a reader must guess an entity, comparator or inferential link. For a whole manuscript use a reverse outline and check Introduction promises against executed work and the final answer. Use selected [section criteria](../scientific-writing/references/section-specific-guidance.md), [object diagnosis](../scientific-writing/references/object-and-continuity.md) and [chapter dependencies](../scientific-writing/references/chapter-contracts.md) rather than repeating their rules here.

Separate scientific defects, reader-understanding defects, official requirements and optional style. Check source support, data roles, adequacy at the claim's scale, fairness and inferential scope. Theory needs appropriate assumptions and derivation/proof; a proposal keeps benefit prospective; exposition need not invent a research gap. Execution success, calibration and polished language do not supply independent validation.

Inspect figures by their reader decision, control/comparator, panel sequence and inference, using [evidence allocation](../scientific-writing/references/argument-distillation.md#evidence-medium). Read the caption, body and rendered display together, including adverse cases. When contribution is weak, name the missing distinction and feasible discriminating work using [the supplement loop](../scientific-writing/references/evidence-supplement.md). Assess whether it would change understanding. Preserve negative returns; a favorable outcome is not an acceptance requirement.

For source-informed revision inspect [retrieval and original context](../scientific-writing/references/library-use.md#selection-contract) and [meaning preservation](../scientific-writing/references/argument-distillation.md#language-and-trimming). Read inverse/selection checks only for tasks involving them. Successful retrieval, filled slots and software tests cannot establish prose quality.

Return each consequential finding with its passage, original evidence/locator, effect on the claim or reader, and smallest adequate repair. Rebuild the connected argument when word substitution would preserve confusion; accept a local edit when sufficient. Route missing facts, domain adequacy and competing explanations to appropriate expertise within scope. Recheck repaired text with neighboring passages before closing findings. Stop optional polish when it no longer improves understanding. See [review-and-handoffs.md](../scientific-writing/references/review-and-handoffs.md) for tracked returns.
