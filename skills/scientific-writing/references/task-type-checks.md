# Conditional scientific operation checks

Read the relevant operation only when present in the actual task. These are editorial checks on scientific claims; chapter labels do not trigger them. Examples informed by the consulted entries retain provenance in [distillation-source-ledger.json](distillation-source-ledger.json).

## Inverse candidates and constraints

One-to-many inverse outputs are candidates unless uniqueness is established. Sampling does not prove complete coverage, global optimality, physical validity or manufacturing feasibility. Distinguish surrogate predictions from independent forward recomputation. State what quantity/domain a constraint acts on and whether it is a soft penalty or explicit operation; constraining one quantity does not enforce all governing dynamics. A source's suggested screening remains prospective until actually executed.

## Selection and optimization budgets

For best-of-N report N and the selection rule. Compare equal selection budgets or explain their difference. Minimum, mean, quantile and success fraction answer different questions. If only retained candidates are rechecked, do not generalize to all generated candidates. A finite screen cannot establish mathematical uniqueness, even if it finds one match.

## Factors and causal interpretation

For crossed designs compare a factor at relevant levels of its partner before generalizing direction or additivity. Joint changes do not isolate component effects. Different perturbation magnitudes rank tested changes rather than intrinsic importance. Several quantities derived from one field are not independent validation observations. An observational association retains possible confounding. Use a proposed mechanism's discriminating diagnostic and preserve alternatives surviving it.

## Generalization and data roles

Distinguish unseen input identity, unseen response range and unseen physical conditions. Keep calibration, training, model selection and held-out testing separate. A numerical benchmark assesses its specified property; agreement with a fitted endpoint does not independently assess physical transfer. Identify the claim and evidence needed rather than demanding every check in every study.
