# Section decisions for scientific writing

Original editorial synthesis informed by MIT, Manchester, UNC and PLOS teaching. Source identities and limits remain in [source-ledger.md](source-ledger.md) and [cohesion-source-ledger.md](cohesion-source-ledger.md). These are reader responsibilities, not journal rules. Read assigned anchors and [paragraph examples](paragraph-examples.md).

<a id="continuity"></a>

## Position and the reading route

Judge the passage by its job and what adjoining text establishes. A section opening may need a scientific premise; the first substantive paragraph may already supply it. A heading or subsection list cannot explain why analysis is needed. A paragraph advances a controlling point and may close with recognition, a condition or unresolved distinction without another gap. Use [object-and-continuity.md](object-and-continuity.md) for object identity and transition diagnosis.

<a id="title"></a>

## Title: object, relationship and scope

Name the studied object and relation plainly, retaining the condition that prevents a consequential misreading. Effect, association, prediction and validation commit to different evidence. Negative findings or applicability assessments need not promise improvement. Check the grammatical core and ambiguous modifiers; confirm unusual terminology in closest originals. Trim while subject and scope remain clear.

<a id="abstract"></a>

## Abstract: a self-contained answer

Derive the final abstract from the complete argument. Its opening makes the entity/phenomenon and concrete difficulty recoverable, through background or a direct study action. State objective, decisive answering route, grouped findings and supported implication in an order fitting article and venue. Explain what the comparison distinguishes instead of listing the execution itinerary. A procedural detail earns space when it changes the finding, data role or scope; other definitions remain in the body. Select numerical anchors for the understanding they add, with comparator, units and conditions. A purpose is not efficacy and an unevaluated design is not measured benefit. Readers should recover question, route, answer and boundary without decoding a new noun chain.

<a id="introduction"></a>

## Introduction: previous achievement to present question

Choose background through contribution and evidence route. Numerical work may need model assumptions/predictions; experiments manipulation/sampling distinctions; theory known results and assumption roles; synthesis competing interpretations and evidence scope. Admit what closest work resolves, then identify the source-supported aspect it leaves undecidable for this purpose. Exposition can explain a known relationship without claiming a field-level gap.

Synthesize sources around that distinction rather than list neighboring methods. Each paragraph leaves a recognition the next uses; express a bridge where it would otherwise be guessed. Derive the closing objective and approach from the tension, making a promise the work fulfills. Separate objective from knowledge/capability actually earned. Preserve a necessary aim or rationale without repeating abstract findings. If no meaningful distinction is supported, use [constructive supplementation](evidence-supplement.md), not invented novelty.

<a id="methods"></a>

## Methods: why this design answers the question

Explain why the study unit, scale, selection or assumptions let the question be examined. Define consequential quantities, conditions, procedures, comparison rules and evidence roles. At equations or unfamiliar procedures make the need, formulation/source or derivation, assumptions and use understandable. Model introduction follows the shared object method. Name schemes before codes; distinguish measured inputs, numerical controls, fitted values and observation rules.

Define parameters at usable locations with units, role, values and source/choice rationale as needed. Repeat values for changed comparisons, not ritual. Explain what changed, what stayed common and what alternatives remain inseparable. Methods states how adequacy is checked; evidence reports actual outcomes. Numerical claims may need convergence/benchmarks, measurements controls/reliability, physical transfer independent observations. Theory orders assumptions and derivations. Checks depend on the claim. Put routine paths/debugging in retrievable reproduction material while retaining decisive assumptions in the argument.

<a id="results-discussion-conclusions"></a>

## Results, discussion and conclusions: evidence to understanding

Organize evidence by questions and dependencies. Give the purpose of a defined comparison, consequential observation/uncertainty, and supported answer. Body prose carries judgments; captions identify displays and reading conditions. Move to another factor/diagnostic because a prior result leaves a distinction unresolved, not to accumulate outputs. Interpret named evidence with comparable literature and surviving alternatives. A pattern can support an explanation without uniquely identifying its mechanism.

Use [comparison checks](argument-distillation.md#comparison-and-inference) for fairness/scope and [evidence allocation](argument-distillation.md#evidence-medium) for purposeful displays. Combined Results and Discussion can keep local inference beside evidence; a separate Discussion adds cross-result synthesis, literature comparison, explanation or common limits. Printed architecture is independent of authorship.

Conclude with what evidence changes about the question, integrating results that change the same judgment. Preserve contrary outcomes and alternatives. Conditional applications and future tests stay conditional. If interpretation needs absent evidence, identify the discriminating question instead of strengthening wording. Length follows the reasoning needed, not a quota of paragraphs, numbers or displays.
