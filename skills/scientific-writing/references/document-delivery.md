# Editable document delivery and format fidelity

Use this method when an existing manuscript must also be supplied in Word or another editable format. Preserve the accepted manuscript; conversion changes representation, not scientific wording or evidence.

Identify what fidelity means for the request: content, semantic editability, visual appearance and pagination are separate properties. Do not promise lossless editable PDF conversion. A full-page image can preserve appearance but cannot stand in for editable paragraphs, tables or equations. If the choice is consequential and unclear, ask once and continue inspecting the available sources.

When the original structured source is available, generate the editable document from it and use the PDF as the visual reference. Retain actual citation order, equation and object numbers, cross-references, symbols, units, table values, figure identities and captions. Prefer native Word equations and tables for editable requests. Identify unsupported structures rather than silently dropping them or turning the whole document into images. Preserve the figure's data and aspect ratio; disclose any representation change that matters for editing or resolution.

For substantive conversions the coordinator gives a production owner the accepted source, rendered reference, requested editing capability and owned output path. An independent review owner compares actual outputs with these inputs. Writing owners resolve only missing or ambiguous source structure; they do not rewrite accepted prose to make a converter succeed. The coordinator closes the handoff with a factual account of what was preserved and what differs.

Verify both content and rendering. Check equation structure and symbols, numerical tables, captions, citations and references; render the Word document with an available actual engine and inspect all pages for missing content, clipping, broken formulas or tables, figure resolution and pagination drift. Count discrepancies by their meaning, not just file size or successful conversion. Text comparison alone cannot certify mathematical or visual equivalence. If an engine or exact layout is unavailable, preserve the editable output and report the remaining uncertainty; do not label an unrendered document verified.

Repair concrete omissions or layout defects in the conversion source, regenerate and reread the affected output. Stop optional spacing adjustments when the document is complete, readable and meets the requested fidelity; do not spend unlimited effort forcing identical pages between different typesetting engines. Deliver the editable file with a short explanation of material differences. Keep scientific state, the accepted PDF and external publication status unchanged unless a separate authorized revision requires otherwise.
