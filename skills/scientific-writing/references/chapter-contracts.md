# Chapter responsibilities and evidence-dependent writing

This is the project's coordination method, synthesized from MIT_INTRODUCTION, MIT_ABSTRACT, MIT_METHODS, PLOS_STRUCTURE and UNC_PARAGRAPHS in [source-ledger.md](source-ledger.md). It is not a journal rule or a requirement for a particular team size. For a major revision use it before separate chapter assignments; for a small edit use only the affected dependency.

## One argument, several responsibilities

The scientific editor owns the common argument: research object and scope → closest established knowledge → specific unresolved aspect or explanatory need → design or argument that addresses it → evidence or derivation → bounded answer. Select the motivation by the contribution and article type. Original research needs a source-supported unresolved aspect, which can concern applicability, a distinguishable explanation or a previously conflated observable rather than a new algorithm. An expository note can clarify a standard result for its intended readers without claiming a field-level gap or new derivation. Identify actual predecessor limitations when claiming them; do not infer a literature gap from a convenient set of saved runs.

Give each writer a short shared packet: current central answer, evidence type, scope, object/comparator definitions, decisive source locators, actual results and contrary evidence, adjacent section's outgoing/incoming point, owned paths and return condition. Long context should hold necessary originals and reasoning, rather than the entire conversation. A worker returns complete usable text plus the few unresolved dependencies that affect it.

Where adjacent assignments overlap, agree on the main location for a full explanation or definition and the distinct job of its next appearance. Include only this consequential division in the same packet.

When journal/source learning is assigned, return a role-specific learning result before dependent drafting: verified current author instructions for the article type, identified comparable papers/passages actually read, applicable requirements, observed conventions and justified adaptation decisions. Source work supplies research lineage and methodological comparisons; Methods learning supplies definition/design/detail choices; outcome learning supplies evidence units, interpretation and display roles; editorial review tests their use in the integrated paper. These are responsibilities that can be combined, not mandatory separate agents. The coordinator interprets the return and shares only the needed originals, locators and adjacent argument. Make the author guide, available reference full texts and manuscript retrievable for authorized human assessment; distinguish abstract-only access and unavailable originals. Record human review as requested, pending or received according to actual events, and use actual feedback rather than inventing approval from access or silence.

| Responsibility | Input needed | What the return must establish | Dependency to close |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sources and research lineage | Closest original studies and actual design | Which object/aspect is established, conditional or unresolved; definitions and populations | Editor accepts or narrows the source-backed gap, or confirms the article's explanatory need, before writers use it |
| Methods author | Question, equations/procedures, study-unit rationale, data roles and executed checks | Design defines consequential quantities, conditions, baselines, observation rules and assessment roles | Evidence author confirms that reported comparisons follow this design |
| Results and local interpretation author | Defined design, actual outputs, adequacy evidence, closest comparisons and counterevidence | Each evidence unit answers a scientific question and explains what the observed comparison establishes | Methods author checks quantities/design; interpretation author checks evidence and inference |
| Introduction writer | Accepted lineage plus evidence-author and discussion returns | Reader can infer why this specific work is needed and why its route addresses the unresolved aspect or explanatory need | Editor checks promises against the actual evidence/argument and Conclusions |
| Discussion and conclusions author | Results, closest factor-specific literature and counterevidence | Evidence changes a named understanding; alternatives and application limits are explicit | Conclusion answers the introductory tension without adding an untested mechanism |
| Title and abstract author | Current complete argument and supported answer | Subject/relationship/scope and distinctive contribution are clear to intended readers | Final cross-check against Introduction, Results and Conclusions |
| Whole-manuscript reviewer | Actual integrated source, figures, definitions and source passages | Reader continuity, scientific support and section responsibilities survive integration | Editor inspects repaired text and closes consequential findings |

Choose writing ownership independently of printed architecture. One writer can handle a tightly coupled design and its outcomes; two owners can also be appropriate: one writes Methods, while the other writes integrated Results and Discussion. Both retain scientific accountability for the design–evidence relation. Before integration they reciprocally check the consequential quantity definitions, units, conditions, changed/held factors, baselines, observation rules, data roles and evidence locators. The Methods owner checks whether the reported comparison follows the executed design; the outcome owner checks whether Methods defines what the evidence and interpretation need. Record only discrepancies or unresolved dependencies in the existing handoff, not a second mandatory form. Expertise, dependency and workload determine the allocation; neither one owner per heading nor a common Methods–Results owner is universal.

For MAF's existing writing metadata, an owner of combined Results and Discussion selects both `methods_results` and `discussion_conclusions`; the task's assigned paths and purpose define the actual prose ownership. A Methods-only task can select `methods_results` while owning only Methods. No new section enum or chapter scheduler is needed.

Adjacent-section owners exchange the outgoing point, the incoming new work and the main explanation location along with the actual boundary passages. Each reads both sides: the preceding section must prepare the next, and the next must use that premise while adding its assigned information. The editor checks the integrated boundary as well as scope and definition consistency. Clear standalone sections and matching summaries do not establish complementarity. Record only a located mismatch or unresolved dependency in the existing handoff.

An Introduction writer can also produce title/abstract later, provided the final input is the whole paper. A working abstract can guide early planning; final title and abstract must reflect the actual evidence and bounded conclusion. Separate a reviewer from the prose it judges where feasible; disclose prior exposure and same-model review. New scientific evidence can reopen the shared argument; optional phrasing changes cannot silently enlarge the contribution.

## Introduction follows the source of evidence

The closing paragraph follows from the specific unresolved aspect or explanatory need appropriate to the article: establish the objective, identify the reason for the chosen approach, then outline the main evidence-producing or explanatory route. Use a transition only when the preceding argument warrants it. It should tell the reader how the question will be answered or clarified; the abstract reports the answer. It need not reproduce the abstract's result list or give every setting.

| Evidence type | Background needed to derive the question | Bridge to the present work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Experimental | What is observed; which sample/state/condition makes competing explanations or measurement ambiguity consequential; what previous experiments resolved | Explain why the chosen manipulation and measurements distinguish the unresolved aspect, with relevant controls or sampling logic |
| Numerical/modeling | What predictions are needed; successes and limits of the closest models; assumptions, calibration/observation choices or regimes that leave a specific prediction uncertain | Explain what model change or comparison isolates that uncertainty and what evidence assesses its consequence; avoid a catalogue of solvers |
| Theoretical | Known result and assumptions; the unresolved regime/property/implication for original research, or the assumption roles and consequences that an expository note needs to clarify | State the assumptions and argument or derivation being developed, whether standard or new, and the consequence to be established or clarified; numerical examples do not substitute for a proof |
| Mixed | Why one evidence source alone cannot answer the stated problem; what each source constrains | Explain the complementary roles and their common quantities/populations; do not equate a fitted observation with independent validation |
| Review/synthesis | Established competing interpretations and the scope of evidence that can adjudicate them | State the synthesis basis and the comparison or organizing insight, rather than invent an experimental gap |

These are prioritization questions, not templates or paragraph counts. Several tensions may be present, but each must contribute to the main question. Admit prior success before defining its remaining limit. A paragraph can close with a supported recognition or scope condition; it need not announce a new gap.

## Headings, openings and the reverse outline

For major revision, extract the full section/subsection list and one controlling statement per substantive paragraph. Read that outline without the prose. It should expose a research route: problem → design and defined observables → decisive comparisons → explanation → answer. A heading should name a disciplinary object and its relation or role; avoid arbitrary branch names, execution verbs and attractive but undefined concepts. Confirm unusual combinations in the closest originals. Generic conventional headings remain useful when their contents have clear responsibilities.

A section or subsection needs a premise when readers cannot yet tell why this unit, quantity or comparison is needed, what preceding result it depends on, or how the next analysis follows. Supply that scientific relationship in the opening or immediately connected prose; a heading alone seldom supplies an unexplained design rationale. A separate opening paragraph is unnecessary when the first paragraph already makes the relation clear. Do not replace this premise with a list of subsections. Observe actual comparable articles before claiming a venue convention.

Assign recurring concepts complementary work across sections. An Introduction establishes why a question and broad route are needed; Methods makes that route assessable through the actual design, definitions and procedures; Results supplies the answer and its support; Discussion and Conclusions develop the understanding and scope earned. Adapt these jobs to the article type. Keep a first mention understandable even when the full explanation belongs later. At the next appearance, ask what needed detail, evidence, qualification or synthesis it adds. Brief recall can orient readers, retain an essential condition or support a self-contained section; repeated terminology alone is not a defect. Nearby passages that restate the same rationale or route at the same level need consolidation, relocation or a more concrete next step.

Methods prose connects need → design choice → definition → what the definition makes assessable. Results connects comparison purpose → observation and magnitude → evidence-supported inference → next consequential comparison. Provide sources for borrowed choices and locally derived identities where needed. Avoid an execution diary, unexplained symbols, repetitive disclaimers or transitions that imply causality from association.

## Evidence units and conditional architecture

Decide what interpretive work the paper needs. A separate Discussion earns its place through cross-result synthesis, explanatory comparison, implications or limits that need development. A combined Results and Discussion is useful when local evidence and its meaning are best read together and a later chapter would repeat them. Either architecture preserves the distinction between observation and inference. Do not put all meaningful interpretation several pages away merely to comply with a default outline.

Each substantive unit should let readers recover its purpose, the defined comparison, actual evidence and uncertainty, the supported answer and the reasoning needed to interpret it. These can span several paragraphs and displays; they are not a fixed sentence recipe. In combined sections, place explanatory and literature comparisons beside the evidence they explain. In separate sections, Results still states the bounded answer earned by the observation; extended explanation returns to named results and locators. Captions identify displays, conditions and measurements, while body prose carries the question and consequential observation/inference. Self-contained captions do not excuse caption-only results.

Check the actual study unit: why does the chosen sample, domain, condition, dataset, mathematical object or synthesis population answer this question? Explain what its scale, selection or assumptions make distinguishable, and what transfer requires. A generic claim that the field matters or that a case is convenient does not establish its scientific role. A representative or idealized unit need not stand for all possible units; its justified role determines the claim.

## Order evidence by its dependencies

Ask what must already be established for the next inference to be credible. Define observables and comparison rules before using them. Put a necessary check's actual outcome before, or clearly alongside, the finding that depends on it; Methods explains the assessment design and Results reports what the assessment found. If venue structure moves detail elsewhere, give a precise evidence reference at the point of reliance. A successful run or a planned check is not a reported adequacy result.

The prerequisites depend on the claim: numerical accuracy may need convergence or a benchmark; a measurement inference may need calibration, controls or reliability; transfer to a physical system may need an appropriate independent physical comparison. These are conditional evidence questions, not a compulsory test list. A theoretical manuscript orders assumptions, lemmas, derivations and consequences by logical dependence and does not need invented experimental validation. When a prerequisite is absent, name the affected inference and return the smallest discriminating question, with a cheaper check or a narrower defensible claim where available. Keep the actual evidence status visible.

## Acceptance and efficient return

Before integration the editor checks that the Introduction's promise, the design's comparison, the Results' answer and the Conclusions' implication refer to the same object and scope. Return a short dependency note only where a relation was changed or remains unresolved; do not make every sentence a bureaucratic record. The reviewer supplies the reverse outline and located counterexamples, not just a score.

Two rounds that only exchange synonyms call for reassessing the object, source or argument. Ask a delayed agent whether it is checking sources, reasoning or repeatedly polishing before interrupting it. Continue necessary thinking/source work; request the best complete candidate and unresolved questions when repeated editing is the cause. Further revision follows another agent's concrete feedback.
