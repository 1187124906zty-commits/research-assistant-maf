# Argument distillation from audited research prose

Original editorial synthesis, not mandatory phrases or a source quotation. [distillation-source-ledger.json](distillation-source-ledger.json) retains consulted entries, actual chapters, locators and transfer limits. An editorial abstraction cannot support a new study's results.

<a id="reader-task"></a>

## Start from the reader's decision

Ask what the reader must understand or assess at this position: question, design, evidence, explanation or implication. Identify the actual proposition and located support before selecting wording. Keep the product and scope: revision uses supplied evidence, exposition explains knowledge, a proposal states intended operations. For objects/transitions use the shared [object method](object-and-continuity.md); for section jobs use [section guidance](section-specific-guidance.md). Source-learning mechanics belong in [library-use.md](library-use.md#selection-contract).

<a id="language-and-trimming"></a>

## Choose language and remove redundancy without changing science

Restate propositions and their relation before selecting a connector. A contrast needs a common dimension and a consequence a present premise. Check temporal ambiguity in “while” or “since” when it matters. Choose verbs by claim and conditions: a tested trend can be demonstrated while its mechanism remains only compatible with observations. There is no universal strength ranking for “suggest”, “support” and “demonstrate” detached from their objects.

Trim reporting shells and nominalized operations when direct subjects/verbs preserve meaning. Compare before and after for object, action, comparator, conditions, data role, uncertainty, modality and provenance. Retain distinctions that change inference, including adverse findings. Locate relocated evidence rather than erase it. Technical collocations supply terminology, not support.

<a id="source-synthesis"></a>

## Synthesize sources around an unresolved decision

Group predecessors by question, capability, assumption or information requirement. Assess their conclusions against design/data and explain the remaining distinction relevant here. Keep actual source chapter and proposed target separate. A Discussion proposal stays prospective when adapted into a plan; one study's limitation does not prove a field-wide gap. Investigate conflicts among abstract, table and body instead of adopting the strongest wording.

<a id="comparison-and-inference"></a>

## Preserve the comparison that gives a finding meaning

Identify compared objects, varied/retained conditions, response, source and aggregation/selection. Equal nominal inputs may produce unequal realized states. Preserve calibration and held-out data roles. Match units, budgets and uncertainty when they affect fairness; explain unequal conditions instead of silently calling comparisons equivalent. Distinguish observation from cause and tested conditions from general applicability. A planned check remains planned; independent evidence is not a relabeled training fit.

Test a consequential assertion against an adverse case consistent with the evidence. If that case would falsify it and is not ruled out, investigate or narrow the assertion. Read [task-type-checks.md](task-type-checks.md) when inverse candidates, best-of-N selection, physical constraints or causal/factorial inference actually arise. These checks are conditional on the scientific operation, not the manuscript chapter.

<a id="evidence-medium"></a>

## Allocate evidence between prose, displays and appendix

Start with the reader decision, then choose its carrier: prose for reasoning, tables for exact comparable quantities, plots for relationships/distributions, schematics for difficult spatial/procedural relations. For each figure determine purpose → control/comparator → panels → interpretation. What question does it answer, what makes the contrast fair, what order/scale lets the reader see it, and what can the actual evidence establish? A panel earns space through distinct information. Figure count and visual similarity to an exemplar are not quality criteria.

Where spatial or temporal structure carries the claim, consider a direct matched-scale view before summary metrics; a metric plot remains primary when it carries the decisive evidence. Show actual measurements/calculated fields with provenance, consistent scales, units and condition labels. An illustration cannot substitute for unavailable data. Keep contrary cases and uncertainty visible. Read special cases only when needed.

Put evidence essential to the principal inference near that result; place extended checks and routine detail in retrievable supplements. Body prose states the comparison, finding and consequence rather than every panel value. Captions define reading conditions. Inspect rendered proximity, legibility, caption/body repetition and whether central counterevidence is hidden. Merge fragments sharing one point without making unreadable blocks. Review revised prose and displays together.
