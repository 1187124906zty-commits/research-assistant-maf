# Scientific Writing Core

Start with the assigned product, audience and question. Work from actual sources and results, including failures. Establish what the paper adds: a finding, explanation, method, applicability boundary or useful synthesis. Exposition can clarify established knowledge without claiming novelty. Preserve the user's scope and evidence roles; fluency does not establish scientific support.

Develop a paragraph around something the reader needs to understand. Use recognizable scientific objects and verbs to explain their relations. Read adjoining paragraphs together: what does the first establish, and why does the next follow? Explain a missing premise before choosing a connector. Keep conditions that change the inference while moving routine execution detail to retrievable reproduction material. Precision should help readers follow the science rather than make every sentence carry the full protocol.

For explicit writing assignments, MAF supplies the short [object method](object-and-continuity.md), selected [section guidance](section-specific-guidance.md), [argument checks](argument-distillation.md) and [illustrative paragraphs](paragraph-examples.md). They support decisions rather than prescribe openings or paragraph counts. Other hosts read only applicable sections. Read [source-use.md](source-use.md) for source/citation repairs. Current author instructions establish official venue requirements.

When the contribution or its evidence is missing, use [constructive supplementation](evidence-supplement.md): identify the unresolved decision, seek a feasible comparison or derivation that could distinguish alternatives, and use the actual return to revise the argument. A useful negative result can answer the question. Continue unaffected writing while the dependent claim remains pending.

Use [library-use.md](library-use.md#selection-contract) when a source library is supplied or source learning requested. Read the original passage and adjacent context before transferring its move; distinguish observed source position from a recommended target. Optional source-backed exemplars retain original locators and transfer limits. Bundled paragraph examples are explicitly illustrative, never source quotations or evidence for the user's study.

Read [chapter-contracts.md](chapter-contracts.md) for substantial integration, [review-and-handoffs.md](review-and-handoffs.md) for tracked repairs and [document-delivery.md](document-delivery.md) for editable conversion. [Institutional provenance](institution-guidance.md), [source ledger](source-ledger.md) and [cohesion ledger](cohesion-source-ledger.md) remain available when attribution or teaching basis matters. Load detail to answer an actual question.

Deliver complete assigned prose and located unresolved issues that affect it. Reread the actual artifact: can the intended reader recover the question, decisive reasoning/comparison, answer and boundary? Software checks verify delivery paths; manuscript review judges scientific and reader outcomes.

The [source-backed starter cards](source-backed-exemplars.md) preserve DOI, actual paragraph/figure locations, authored context analysis and transfer conditions. Explicit Introduction, Methods/Results and Discussion scopes receive one relevant card each; other cards stay optional. These few papers establish inspected examples, not general journal conventions. Adaptations on the cards are authored hypothetical paragraphs.
