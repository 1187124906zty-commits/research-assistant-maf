# Methods and Results

Explain how the question is answered: model/design, assumptions, data or experimental units, conditions, variables/QoIs, procedures, comparisons and relevant adequacy checks. Include information needed to assess validity and reproduce the substantive work. Separate physical properties, numerical settings and fitted/calibrated parameters. Attribute borrowed equations and label local derivations/extensions.

Choose ownership by expertise and evidence dependencies. A Methods owner and a separate owner of combined Results and Discussion can share accountability by reciprocally checking consequential quantities, units, conditions, data roles, baselines, observation rules and evidence locators. The Methods owner checks the reported design; the outcome owner checks that Methods supplies the definitions and assessment rationale it uses. A common owner is also appropriate when useful. Printed architecture does not determine team size. Coordination details are in [chapter contracts](../scientific-writing/references/chapter-contracts.md).

Apply [the compact methods criteria](../scientific-writing/references/section-specific-guidance.md#methods) for decision rationale, formula identity, parameters defined once and scientific schemes before case codes; for results, apply [the evidence responsibilities](../scientific-writing/references/section-specific-guidance.md#results-discussion-conclusions). MAF directly supplies these portions for this scope; other hosts should read them.

Put essential scientific definitions and justification where the reader needs them. Detailed file paths, software environment and routine implementation can move to reproduction material when retrievable and not needed to evaluate the method. Do not hide a decisive assumption or data role in an appendix. Preserve calibration versus independent validation and the actual evidence level.

Explain why the actual study unit, scale or selection lets the design answer the question; a convenient case and broad application importance do not supply this rationale. Organize evidence by scientific dependencies: actual checks needed to trust a later inference appear before or alongside it, with a located reference when details are elsewhere. Methods states how checks were made; Results states their actual outcomes. Planned checks, successful execution, calibration and independent validation retain their distinct status. Theoretical work orders assumptions and derivations without a compulsory experiment.

Organize results by the scientific question and useful comparisons. Connect each unit's analysis premise and purpose to the decisive observations, effect scale, uncertainty, contrary evidence and bounded answer. Body prose must carry consequential observations and their evidence-supported meaning; self-contained captions do not replace this work. Do not duplicate every displayed number or turn a displayed pattern into a mechanism proof. Make figure/table references precise and ensure units, conditions and sample/replication definitions are clear.

Choose separate or combined Results and Discussion according to the interpretive work and actual venue requirements. Local evidence and interpretation can form an integrated unit; a separate Discussion should add cross-result synthesis rather than repeat it. Keep observations distinguishable from inferences and connect every explanation to the evidence it explains. When assigned a combined section in MAF, select both existing `methods_results` and `discussion_conclusions` scopes; assigned paths delimit ownership. For a design/theory manuscript without empirical evaluation, write the specified method and boundaries; omit fabricated result passages and measured performance claims.

When venue learning is assigned, read current author instructions and comparable papers for this article type; return source locators, applicable requirements, observed Methods/evidence conventions and justified adaptation decisions to the coordinator. Share the relevant originals/locators, not an entire historical bibliography in every writing context.

Read [source-use.md](../scientific-writing/references/source-use.md) for method provenance and [argument-and-readers.md](../scientific-writing/references/argument-and-readers.md) for detail placement. Return the assigned sections and exact missing data/check questions, without conducting new experiments unless the task authorizes them.
