The previous guides already addressed synthesis and section responsibilities. R5 adds small executable diagnoses and ensures that scoped requests receive them:

- [Object and continuity method](../../skills/scientific-writing/references/object-and-continuity.md): original entity/population, comparator, subject/action, antecedent and distinct sentence/paragraph checks.
- [Chapter dependencies](../../skills/scientific-writing/references/chapter-contracts.md): evidence-type Introduction, accountable Methods–Results ownership, whole-paper title/abstract and reverse-outline review.
- [Located source record](../../skills/scientific-writing/references/cohesion-source-ledger.md): actual Duke, Purdue and UNC passages/read scopes, licensing and unavailable-original limits.

`guidance.load_role` directly includes the compact object method for an explicit `writing` scope, and the chapter method for coordinators or `full_manuscript`. Other detailed references remain progressively loaded. Existing unscoped calls retain their smaller prompts and original saved calls retain their historical instructions. [Usage](../../docs/writing-skills.zh.md) explains the optional task selection and role routing.

Realistic forward application used the actual exported loader output in same-task Codex specialists: one source/evidence author revised Methods–Results, an architecture author revised Introduction, the coordinator integrated the paper and a separate reviewer inspected actual text and PDF. The writers exchanged definitions and promises. The review found concrete defects, including confusion between a class mean and one track, between pointwise specific heat and integrated enthalpy reductions, and between fixed source fraction and fixed power across operating conditions. Actual repair and recheck remain located in the R5 case records.
